Chili Grow Planner

Everything you need to grow hot chili from seed to harvest, plus a calendar to log every watering, fertilizer and insecticide, with costs added up for you.

Growing details

Germination7–21 days at 25–30 °C. Super-hot types can take longer.
TransplantingAfter 4–6 weeks, when seedlings have 6–8 true leaves.
SpacingAbout 60 cm between rows and 45 cm between plants.
SoilLoose, well-drained, rich in compost. pH 6.0–6.8.
WaterDeep watering 2–3 times a week. Never leave roots waterlogged.
FertilizerCompost at planting, then nitrogen early and potash at flowering and fruiting.
Common pestsAphids, thrips, mites and whitefly. Check leaf undersides weekly.
Harvest90–120 days from sowing, or 120–150 days for super-hot varieties.
